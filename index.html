hi cutie,

We did the big loud two year thing last month, so this one gets to be small and ours, which is honestly my favorite kind. Just us, a normal month, one more little marker on the wall. And what a month to land on, because you started your new job.

I want you to hear this plainly. That old job took more from you than it ever gave, and you carried it a long time without letting it turn you bitter. Then you did the hard, quiet, brave thing and left. I did not do that for you. You did. I just got the best seat in the house, watching you finally choose yourself.

I know the first weeks of anything new come with nerves, the where do I sit, who do I ask, am I doing this right kind of nerves. You are going to be so good at this. Not because it is easy, but because you are the person who shows up scared and does it anyway. I have watched you do that for two years and a month now, and it still gets me every time.

The miles are still the hard part, I will not pretend they are not. But we are the closest we have ever been to done with them, and until then nothing about this new chapter of yours does it alone. Whatever I am carrying stays mine. Your good days stay yours. I just want to see you happy in work that finally fits, coming home lighter, telling me the small stuff about your day.

Two years, one month, one brand new beginning. Go be great, cutie. I will be right here, loud and proud and completely yours.
